Hello! I am **Yibo Zhou**, a Ph.D. candidate in Artificial Intelligence at the
School of Computer Science and Engineering, Beihang University, Beijing, China.
I expect to receive my Ph.D. degree in June 2026.

My research focuses on **computer vision** and **machine learning**, with
particular interests in pedestrian attribute recognition, balanced learning,
out-of-distribution detection, spurious correlation, and robust representation
learning. My work has been published in venues including CVPR, ICML, IJCAI,
IEEE TPAMI, and IJCV.

Before joining Beihang University, I worked as an Artificial Intelligence
Algorithm Engineer at OPPO, where I was affiliated with the 101 Laboratory and
the Imaging Algorithm Development Department.

I received my M.S. degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of
California San Diego and my B.S. degree in Materials Physics from Lanzhou
University.

Profile

I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Beihang University. My research focuses on computer vision and trustworthy machine learning, with particular interests in pedestrian attribute recognition, balanced multi-label learning, out-of-distribution detection, and spurious correlation analysis. My research has appeared in leading computer vision and machine learning venues, including IEEE TPAMI, IJCV, ICML, IJCAI, and CVPR.

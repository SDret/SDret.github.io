I am Yibo Zhou, a researcher working on Computer Vision and Trustworthy Machine Learning. I received my Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from Beihang University in June 2026. My research focuses on developing reliable machine learning systems that can learn from imbalanced data, mitigate spurious correlations, and generalize robustly under distribution shifts. My research has appeared in leading computer vision and machine learning venues, including IEEE TPAMI, IJCV, ICML, IJCAI, and CVPR.
